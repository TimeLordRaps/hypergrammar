Hyper Inversion [03_hyper_inversion]
Purpose: Visualize the reframe from Chomsky ladder progression to closure-loop cross-sections.

Nodes:
- LAD: Classical Inclusion Ladder (legacy-view)
- SEC: Cross-Sections (reinterpretation)
- LOOP: Closure Loop rooted at □ (topology)
- L: Operator L (operator)
- CONT: Continuation (state)
- CLOSE: Form (resolution)
- OPEN: Open Frame (resolution)
- REL: Relation Filtration (=, ≡, ~) (comparison)
- HG: .hg Proof Chain (artifact)
- TRAIL: Link Map / Source Trail (evidence)

Relations:
- Classical Inclusion Ladder --[reinterpreted as]--> Cross-Sections
- Cross-Sections --[samples of]--> Closure Loop rooted at □
- Closure Loop rooted at □ --[advanced by]--> Operator L
- Operator L --[generates]--> Continuation
- Continuation --[if closes]--> Form
- Continuation --[if non-closure]--> Open Frame
- Relation Filtration (=, ≡, ~) --[organizes comparisons within]--> Closure Loop rooted at □
- Form --[encoded as]--> .hg Proof Chain
- Link Map / Source Trail --[grounds claim provenance]--> Cross-Sections

Notes:
- The inversion changes geometry, not baseline literacy requirements.
- Open frames are revelatory diagnostics, not silent failures.
