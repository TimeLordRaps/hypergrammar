Chomsky Hierarchy [02_chomsky_hierarchy]
Purpose: Visualize containment chain, recognizer intuition, and expressivity-versus-proof-cost tradeoff.

Nodes:
- T3: Type-3 Regular (class)
- T2: Type-2 Context-Free (class)
- T1: Type-1 Context-Sensitive (class)
- T0: Type-0 Unrestricted (class)
- FA: Finite Automaton (machine)
- PDA: Pushdown Automaton (machine)
- LBA: Linear-Bounded Automaton (machine)
- TM: Turing Machine (machine)
- EXP: Expressivity (measure)
- BUR: Verification Burden (measure)

Relations:
- Type-3 Regular --[subset of]--> Type-2 Context-Free
- Type-2 Context-Free --[subset of]--> Type-1 Context-Sensitive
- Type-1 Context-Sensitive --[subset of]--> Type-0 Unrestricted
- Type-3 Regular --[recognized by]--> Finite Automaton
- Type-2 Context-Free --[recognized by]--> Pushdown Automaton
- Type-1 Context-Sensitive --[recognized by]--> Linear-Bounded Automaton
- Type-0 Unrestricted --[recognized by]--> Turing Machine
- Type-3 Regular --[lower]--> Expressivity
- Type-0 Unrestricted --[higher]--> Expressivity
- Expressivity --[typically increases]--> Verification Burden

Notes:
- Containment is strict as a modeling heuristic unless proven otherwise.
- Higher power usually raises parsing and verification costs.
