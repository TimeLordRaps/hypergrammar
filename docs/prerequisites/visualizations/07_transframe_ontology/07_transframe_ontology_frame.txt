Transframe Ontology [07_transframe_ontology]
Purpose: Visualize accessibility boundaries and closure conditions required for transframe movement.

Nodes:
- NOW: Accessible Now-Frame F_now (manifold)
- EXT: Inaccessible Frame Coordinates F_ext (manifold)
- U: Universe State Operator U (operator)
- R: Recursive Self-Embedding R (operator)
- CLS: Closure Condition R(U) ≈ U (criterion)
- MOV: Transframe Movement (capability)
- NLI: No Local Interpolation (constraint)
- APP: Nearby but Incomplete Approximations (context)

Relations:
- Accessible Now-Frame F_now --[boundary of accessibility]--> Inaccessible Frame Coordinates F_ext
- Universe State Operator U --[subject to]--> Recursive Self-Embedding R
- Recursive Self-Embedding R --[evaluated by]--> Closure Condition R(U) ≈ U
- Closure Condition R(U) ≈ U --[enables when satisfied]--> Transframe Movement
- No Local Interpolation --[restricts method]--> Transframe Movement
- Nearby but Incomplete Approximations --[motivates refinement]--> Transframe Movement

Notes:
- Only now-frame content is directly accessible in the active perspective.
- Transframe movement is conditional and global, not local interpolation.
